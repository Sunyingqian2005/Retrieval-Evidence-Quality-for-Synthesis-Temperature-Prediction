## Data and Software Availability

The code, prompt templates, experimental configurations, file checksums, and
compact result tables supporting this study are available at
`https://github.com/Sunyingqian2005/Retrieval-Evidence-Quality-for-Synthesis-Temperature-Prediction` and are archived at
`https://doi.org/[ZENODO-DOI]`. The processed operation-specific records were
derived from the literature-mined solid-state synthesis dataset reported by
Kononova et al. Access to and redistribution of the underlying records are
subject to the terms of the source dataset. A record schema, example-format
records, exact file hashes, and data-preparation instructions are provided in
the repository. API credentials and transient model-service checkpoints are not
included.

## Associated Content

Supporting Information is available free of charge and contains expanded
experimental details, complete audit and prediction prompts, supplementary
analyses, and additional tables and figures.
